Refunds & Cancellations

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Project work (migration, MCP servers)

  • Before work starts: cancel any time and get a 100% refund of your advance.
  • After work starts, before delivery: the advance is refunded after deducting the part of the agreed scope already completed, which I will show you.
  • After delivery: no refund, because the work has been delivered. If something I delivered does not match the agreed scope, I fix it free within 7 days. If I cannot fix it, I refund the amount paid for the affected part.

Managed n8n (monthly)

  • Cancel any time before the next billing date, and you will not be charged again.
  • The current month is not refunded once it has started, unless I failed to provide the service.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method through Cashfree, usually within 5–7 working days.

How to request one

Message me from the Contact page with your name, the payment reference and the reason. I reply within 1 business day.
